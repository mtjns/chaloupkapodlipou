Our cottage

Chaloupka pod lípou

is a place where history meets modern comfort. This stylish cottage is located in the small town of Malé Svatoňovice and is the ideal place for your vacation. Whether you're looking for peace, comfort, or the beauty of nature, you'll find it all here. Amenities

Staying at Chaloupka pod lípou

Chaloupka pod lípou is fully equipped for a comfortable stay. The cottage features a spacious living room with a cozy sofa and fireplace, connected to a fully equipped kitchen with modern appliances including a stove, refrigerator, dishwasher, and coffee maker.

Five bedrooms offer comfortable double and single beds, along with ample storage space. Two bathrooms, each with a shower, toilet, and washing machine. Wi-Fi, a TV, and garden furniture for relaxing on the outdoor terrace are also available. For your comfort, the entire cottage is fitted with central heating and other thoughtful details to make your stay even more enjoyable.
