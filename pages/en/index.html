Our cottage

Our Cottage under the Linden Tree

is a place where history meets modern comfort. This stylish cottage is located in the village of Malé Svatoňovice and is the ideal place for your vacation. Whether you're looking for peace, comfort, or the beauty of nature, you'll find it all here. The cottage is fitted with a modern interior while still preserving the charm of a traditional countryside home.

Amenities

Staying with us

Chaloupka pod lípou is fully equipped for a comfortable stay. Inside you'll find a spacious living room with a comfortable sofa and a wood-burning stove, connected to a fully equipped kitchen with modern appliances including a stove, refrigerator, dishwasher, and coffee maker.

Five bedrooms offer comfortable double and single beds and plenty of storage space. Two bathrooms are equipped with a shower, toilet, and washing machine. Wi-Fi, a TV, and garden furniture for relaxing on the outdoor terrace are also available. For your comfort, the entire cottage has central heating and other thoughtful details to make your stay more enjoyable.
