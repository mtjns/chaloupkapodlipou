Guest book

Information

To meet our legal obligations (the Act on the Registration of Accommodated Persons and the Act on the Residence of Foreign Nationals) and to ensure a smooth stay at Chaloupka pod lípou, we kindly ask you to fill in the details of all accommodated guests.

It takes about 10 minutes to complete. You can share the form link with others in your group so that each family or individual can fill in their details separately. Your progress is saved continuously in your browser, so you won't lose your data.

Your data is stored securely (in accordance with GDPR and other regulations) and will be used solely for reservation purposes and legal obligations. You can find more information in our privacy policy. For questions or to hand over your details in person, contact us at {{ site.email }}.
