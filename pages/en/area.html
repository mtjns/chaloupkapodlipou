Local area

Explore the surroundings

Chaloupka pod lípou is located in the charming town of Malé Svatoňovice, perfect for a peaceful getaway or an active holiday. It's also a great starting point for exploring the beautiful Podkrkonoší region.

Step out and breathe in the outdoors

The area offers plenty of opportunities for walks, hikes, and cycling. Take a trip to Jirásek Rocks, or head up to Žaltman — the highest peak of the Jestřebí Mountains — where you'll enjoy wide, breathtaking views of the whole region.

A town with heart and history

Malé Svatoňovice is best known as the birthplace of the famous writer and playwright Karel Čapek. Right on the town square, you'll find the Čapek Brothers Museum, where you can explore exhibits about their lives and work, as well as original paintings by the renowned Josef Čapek.

Where to explore next?

  • • Adršpašsko-Teplice Rocks – a unique rock formations area around 20 minutes away.
  • • Grandmother's Valley in Ratibořice – a romantic spot made famous by Božena Němcová's novel.
  • • Josefov Fortress, Stachberg Fort, or Náchod Castle – perfect for lovers of history and architecture.
