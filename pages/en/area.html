Local area

Explore the surroundings of our cottage

Chaloupka pod lípou is located in the charming village of Malé Svatoňovice, an ideal place for a peaceful getaway as well as an active holiday. It's also a great starting point for discovering the beauty of Podkrkonoší (the Giant Mountains foothills).

Nature within reach

The area offers plenty of opportunities for walks, hikes, and cycling. Take a trip to the Jirásek Rocks, or climb Žaltman — the highest peak of the Jestřebí Mountains, offering a wide view over the whole region.

A town with soul and history

Malé Svatoňovice is best known as the birthplace of the famous writer and playwright Karel Čapek. Right on the town square you'll find the Čapek Brothers Museum, where you can explore not only an exhibition about their lives and work, but also original paintings by Josef Čapek.

Where else to go?

  • Adršpach–Teplice Rocks — a unique rock town just about 20 minutes away by car.
  • Grandmother's Valley in Ratibořice — a romantic spot made famous by Božena Němcová's novel.
  • Josefov Fortress, the Stachelberg fort, Náchod Castle, Vízmburk Castle, the Bohumír copper mine, or Opočno Château — for lovers of history and architecture.
  • The Žaltman lookout tower, Jestřebí bouda, or Lotrand's Cave — shorter walks nearby, suitable for children too.
  • Dvůr Králové Zoo, the Havlovice sports complex, FotbalGolf Pavlišov, or Wenet Farm — for active recreation and fun with children.
  • The open-air pools in Trutnov, Náchod, and Police nad Metují, or the Rozkoš reservoir — a refreshing dip in the summer months.
  • Paddling the Elbe (Labe) — a water adventure for families and experienced paddlers alike.
