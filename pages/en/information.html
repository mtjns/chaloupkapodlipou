1. General rules

  • Smoking is strictly prohibited in all premises of the cottage, including electronic cigarettes.
  • Please respect quiet hours between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM.
  • Indoor shoes must be worn inside the cottage.
  • Do not move any furniture or equipment, and do not tamper with the electrical system.
  • If anything gets damaged, please report it to us as soon as possible.
{% include info-icon.html name="equipment" %}

2. Equipment & facilities

The control for the living room blinds is located on the left behind the curtain, as is the switch for the outdoor lights.

{% include info-icon.html name="outdoor" %}

3. Outdoor area

  • Garden furniture (sun loungers, umbrellas, etc.) is available during the summer months – please return everything to its place after use.
  • The gas grill is for outdoor use only – please clean it after each use.
  • The fire pit is available for guests to use. Firewood is located next to the fire pit, and roasting forks can be found in the bike shed.
{% include info-icon.html name="keys" %}

4. Check-out & keys

Before departure, please wait for the owner to arrive for personal handover of the keys, unless otherwise agreed.
