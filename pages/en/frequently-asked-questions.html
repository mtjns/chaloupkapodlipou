Frequently Asked Questions

Here you'll find answers to the most common questions about your stay in our cottage.

What are the reservation terms?

You'll find the reservation terms and detailed information on how to book a stay with us on our Reservation page.

Do you need to know our exact arrival time?

Yes, it helps us a lot. We don't have a classic reception desk at the cottage — we usually hand over the keys in person, or leave them for you in a secure lockbox.

Is a deposit paid in advance?

Yes, the deposit is 20 % of the total price, or as individually agreed. The balance is due on arrival. If you cancel the reservation more than 30 days before arrival, we refund the entire deposit paid. If you cancel 30 days or fewer before arrival, the deposit is non-refundable.

Can I request a change to my stay dates?

Yes, a date change is possible by arrangement (though we may no longer have availability, and we may require a new deposit). Please get in touch as soon as possible so we can find another suitable date together.

What should we bring with us?

We recommend packing indoor shoes and, in the summer months, definitely something good for the grill or the fire. Otherwise the cottage is fully equipped — we try to make sure you lack nothing and feel right at home. You'll find the list of what's available to you on our website.

Can you recommend a trip?

The area around the cottage has a lot to offer. You'll find a few tips and our interactive map on our website in the local area section.

Can we bring a dog to the cottage?

Unfortunately, we don't allow dogs or other pets.

Should we bring our own bed linen and towels?

No need — don't worry about a thing. Everything will be waiting for you freshly made up and ready, including hand towels and bath towels.

Is the cottage suitable for small children?

Yes, we're fully prepared for families with children. A big advantage is our safe, fenced grounds around the cottage. Children have plenty of space to play in the fresh air, and you can be sure they won't wander off anywhere. Inside, there's plenty of room for time together and for relaxing. For families with small children we can provide a cot — just let us know in advance.

Have more questions?

If you didn't find the answer to your question, please contact us by email or on WhatsApp.
