Frequently Asked Questions

Here you'll find answers to the most common questions about your stay in our cottage.

What are the reservation terms?

You'll find detailed information about reservation conditions and booking on our Reservation page.

Can I change my dates?

Yes, changes are possible. Please contact us as soon as possible so we can find a suitable alternative date.

Is the cottage suitable for small children?

The cottage features five comfortable bedrooms, well-equipped bathrooms, and a spacious living room. For families with young children, we can provide a crib upon request — please let us know in advance.

Have other questions?

If you didn't find the answer you're looking for, please contact us by email or fill out the form on our reservation page.
